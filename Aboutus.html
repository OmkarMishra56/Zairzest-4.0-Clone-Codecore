Zairza: Igniting Innovation

Zairza, more than just a club, is a student community of tech enthusiasts at Odisha University of Technology and Research. Rooted in the vision "Wonder, Think, Create," we've been pushing the boundaries of innovation in Robotics, Software, and Design since 2006. Officially recognized as a central club in 2017, Zairza has consistently achieved excellence, earning numerous accolades.

Our mission is to empower students with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in their careers. Regular workshops and sessions provide a strong foundation, while industry mentors provide real-world experience. Strategically located in the SAC area, we’re easily accessible to all students.

Beyond technical pursuits, Zairza organizes events and ZairzaFest to celebrate tech. This three-day extravaganza features exciting competitions, workshops, and hackathons. Zairzest 3.0 was a resounding success, attracting hundreds of participants and showcasing the incredible talent of our members.

Join Zairza and be part of a legacy of innovation. Together, we’ll shape the future of technology.
